# TenFore Golf — Email Design System

A living Storybook for the **TenFore Golf** email system. Every email here is a
real React component, built from a shared library of primitives and previewed
across its real-world edge cases — so design, copy, and engineering all work
from one source of truth.

**44 email templates · 34 shared components · 2 partner venues.**

---

## What this is

TenFore Golf is a cloud booking platform for golf venues. This Storybook is the
home for every **transactional and lifecycle email** the platform sends — tee
time confirmations, receipts, membership renewals, gift cards, simulator
bookings, operator notifications, and more.

Instead of static mockups, each email is a composable component. Its variations
(a refund vs. no refund, a manual vs. auto renewal, a single vs. multi-item
order) are captured as **Storybook stories**, so you can see every state the
real system produces.

## The model — platform + partners

The system has three kinds of sender, and that distinction drives the branding:

- **TenFore Golf** — the platform itself (the Crane booking app, accounts, the
  Golf Buddies feature). Platform-branded.
- **Partner venues** — the places you actually book. Two are live today:
  **Sagamore Spring Golf Club** (a golf course) and **FloGolf Lounge** (an
  indoor Golfzon simulator lounge). The venue is the *content*; TenFore is the
  *platform*.
- **Admin** — operator- and finance-facing notifications (a sale was booked, a
  payment failed, an ACH run completed). Internal, not customer-facing.

## Anatomy of an email

Almost every email composes the same skeleton:

```
EmailShell            → neutral canvas + centered 600px white card + inbox preheader
  Header              → EmailHeader (variant="platform", or "admin" for internal)
  Hero / StatusHero   → image hero (with venue logo overlay) or text headline block
  VenueBadge          → which partner this is from (logo + name + address) — below the headline
  EmailSection(s)     → the body: details, payment, callouts, CTAs
  Footer              → EmailFooter (TenFore platform)
```

The headline leads; the venue `VenueBadge` sits beneath it as secondary context.

## Branding rules — "who is this from?"

A deliberate, consistent answer to sender identity:

- **The header logo never changes per venue.** One `EmailHeader` component:
  customer emails use the TenFore platform mark; internal emails pass
  `variant="admin"` for the TenFore Admin mark.
- **The venue is identified in the body**, via a **`VenueBadge`** (venue logo +
  name + address) and/or **hero imagery** with the venue logo overlaid on the
  photo. So a recipient always sees *TenFore the platform* up top and *which
  partner* they booked with in the content.
- **The footer is always the TenFore platform footer.**

## Edge cases — how variants work

The single most important convention: **one template per archetype; edge cases
are story args, not new files.** A `Modification` email is one component; its
"with refund" and "without refund" states are two stories.

Variation runs along a few clear axes:

- **Lifecycle** — confirm → modify → cancel → refund
- **Payment state** — paid online · pay at venue · amount due · refund · failed
- **Status tone** — success (green) · info (blue) · warning (amber), expressed
  through the `Callout` color
- **Audience** — customer · operator · platform-internal
- **Single vs. multi** — one line item vs. grouped line items

A few representative examples you can open right now:

- **Sagamore Golf Club / Membership / Expiring** — *Manual renew* vs. *Auto-renew*
- **Sagamore Golf Club / Orders / Purchase Receipt** — *Pro Shop* vs. *Snack Bar* (with product thumbnails)
- **Sagamore Golf Club / Tee Times / Confirmation** — range-bucket code as a *PIN*, *Barcode*, or *QR*
- **FloGolf Lounge / Simulator Booking / Confirmation** — *Default* vs. *Multiple Bays*
- **Sagamore Golf Club / Gift Cards / Purchase** — *Self* vs. *Gift*

Receipts can carry real product thumbnails — the pro-shop and snack-bar orders
pair each line item with its image:

## One canonical scenario

All defaults flow from `src/lib/scenario.ts` (golfer, course, tee time, payment,
membership, gift card…) plus `src/lib/flogolf.ts` for the simulator venue. Every
template defaults its props from that scenario and lets stories override simple
fields — so the whole system tells one coherent, realistic story, and new edge
cases are expressed as overrides rather than copied data.

## The component library

`Email Components` holds the shared building blocks, including:

- **Structure & layout** — EmailShell, EmailHeader (platform + admin variants),
  EmailFooter, EmailSection, Divider, CTAStack, SupportLine
- **Heroes & identity** — EmailHero, StatusHero, VenueBadge, Logo, IdStamp
- **Content** — DetailCard, DetailRow, ItemizedList (with optional product
  thumbnails), PaymentSummary, StatusBadge, Callout, WhatChanged, BookingCard,
  ForecastCard, GiftCardVisual, RedemptionCode (PIN / barcode / QR), FeatureList,
  Checklist, NumberedSteps, LocationBlock, SectionHeading
- **Operator** — ContactBlock, DataTable
- **App & engagement** — CTAButton, AppBanner, AppStoreBadge, EmailRating

These primitives are genuinely shared, not decorative: `DetailCard` backs 32
emails, `SupportLine` 16, `CTAStack` 12 — change one and every email follows.

## Extending the system

- **Add a partner** → drop their assets in `src/assets/brand/`, add a
  `src/lib/<partner>.ts`, and copy a profile under **TenFore Partners** plus an
  email folder. The header/footer chrome stays the same; only the `VenueBadge`
  and hero imagery change.
- **Add an email** → create `src/emails/<Name>/<Name>.tsx` + `<Name>.stories.tsx`,
  default its props from the scenario, and express edge cases as extra stories.
